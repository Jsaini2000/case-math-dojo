Cheat sheet: fractions, zeros and shortcuts
Fractions → %
Handling zeros
  • Multiply: strip the zeros, multiply the leading digits, then add every zero back. 2.4M × 35 → 24 × 35 = 840, plus 5 zeros = 84M.
  • Divide: cancel zeros on both sides first. 8.4B ÷ 35K → 8.4M ÷ 35 = 240K.
  • K = 10³ · M = 10⁶ · B = 10⁹ · T = 10¹²
  • 1,000 × 1M = 1B · 1M ÷ 1K = 1K
Percent tricks
  • x% of y = y% of x. 8% of 50 = 50% of 8 = 4.
  • Build it from parts: 15% = 10% + 5%; 35% = ⅓ + 1.67%.
  • Reversing a change: divide, don't subtract. +25% then back = ÷1.25 (= × 0.8).
  • Rule of 72: years to double ≈ 72 ÷ growth rate.
  • Compounding: 1.1² = 1.21 · 1.1³ = 1.331 · 1.2² = 1.44 · 1.05³ ≈ 1.158
Case formulas
  • Profit = Revenue − Costs; margin = Profit ÷ Revenue
  • Contribution/unit = Price − Variable cost
  • Breakeven units = Fixed costs ÷ Contribution
  • Payback = Investment ÷ Annual profit
  • EV = EBITDA × multiple
